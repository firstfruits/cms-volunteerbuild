Trip Destinations

Vanuatu

Open For Bookings
Book Now
‍Vanuatu is a South Pacific Ocean nation made up of roughly 80 islands that stretch 1,300 kilometres. Port Vila, is the nation’s capital and economic centre, is on the island of Efate.  Our Volunteer Build teams build resilient concrete block homes and school classrooms designed to withstand Category 5 tropical cyclones replacing wooden and tin structures that have been destroyed in past cyclones.

Cambodia

Open For Bookings
Book Now
Cambodia is a nation of contrasts, where ancient history and modern development coexist. Its people's resilience and spirit of community shine through in the face of adversity, making it a land of hope and opportunity.   In Cambodia, Volunteer Build teams focus on constructing resilient homes for those living in poverty.

Mexico

Open For Bookings
Book Now
‍Mexico is located between the U.S. and Central America and has a diverse landscape of mountains, deserts and jungles. Our Volunteer Build teams build homes for the poor and homeless in the arid colonies of southern Tijuana, as well as feeding the homeless and visiting the forgotten in prison. Join us on our next departure 28th September 2024.

Portugal

View Previous Trips
‍Portugal is a European nation (population of 10 million), with rich cultural history that dates back over a thousand years. Volunteer Build teams will assist with rebuilding an old carpentry workshop into a multi-home building with 6 apartments for refugees from Congo and Cameroon.

Peru

View Previous Trips
Peru is a country in South America that's home to a section of Amazon rainforest and Machu Picchu, an ancient Incan city high in the Andes mountains. The region around Machu Picchu, including the Sacred Valley, Inca Trail and colonial city of Cusco, is rich in archaeological sites. On Peru’s arid Pacific coast is Lima, the capital, with a preserved colonial center and important collections of pre-Columbian art.

Russia

View Previous Trips
Russia extends nearly halfway around the Northern Hemisphere. Russia has a maximum east-west extent of some 9,000 km and a north-south width of 2,500 to 4,000 km. There is an enormous variety of landforms and landscapes, which occur mainly in a series of broad latitudinal belts. Arctic deserts lie in the extreme north, giving way southward to the tundra and then to the forest zones, which cover about half of the country and give it much of its character.

Uganda

View Previous Trips
Uganda is a landlocked country in East Africa whose diverse landscape encompasses the snow-capped Rwenzori Mountains and immense Lake Victoria. Its abundant wildlife includes chimpanzees as well as rare birds. Remote Bwindi Impenetrable National Park is a renowned mountain gorilla sanctuary. Murchison Falls National Park in the northwest is known for its 43m-tall waterfall and wildlife such as hippos.

Fiji

View Previous Trips
Fiji is a country in the South Pacific, an archipelago of more than 300 islands. It's famed for rugged landscapes, palm-lined beaches and coral reefs with clear lagoons. Its major islands, Viti Levu and Vanua Levu, contain most of the population. Viti Levu is home to the capital, Suva, a port city with British colonial architecture.

Nepal

View Previous Trips
Nepal was officially the Federal Democratic Republic of Nepal, is a landlocked country in South Asia. It is mainly situated in the Himalayas, but also includes parts of the Indo-Gangetic Plain.

Tonga

View Previous Trips
Tonga is a group of islands in the South Pacific Ocean, located near Fiji and Samoa. It is home to around 103,000 people who live on 36 of the country’s 170 islands. Tonga is the only Pacific country with a constitutional monarchy – it is known officially as the Kingdom of Tonga.
